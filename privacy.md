# StageNight Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

StageNight does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

Users provide their own lyrics, chord charts, and audio files. This content is processed locally on the user's device.

StageNight does not upload users' song audio, lyrics, chord charts, or other user-provided content to StageNight servers.

Auto-Time processing is performed on-device.

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. StageNight does not receive or store users' payment card information.

StageNight does not require an account or login.
